# ENG4U group site

Our Grade 12 English group site, by Senay Aklilu and Lucas Syfridis (Mr. Freitas, Chaminade, 2026–27). It's a single scene: you're in Plato's cave, the projection on the wall shows the two of us as shadows, and you leave through one of two doorways into our portfolios.

Hand-built HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No framework, no build step. Hosted free on GitHub Pages.

## Files

- `index.html`: all the content
- `assets/site.css`: the look (colours and fonts are at the top)
- `assets/site.js`: the light that follows the pointer, the dust in the doorways, the walk into the light
- `assets/rock*.webp|jpg`: the cave wall, generated by `tools/make_rock.py` (no stock photos)
- Fonts: IM Fell English (an old printing type from the 1600s, Shakespeare's century) and Courier Prime (the screenplay typeface), both from Google Fonts

The quote in "The podcast" is from Plato's *Republic*, Book VII, in Benjamin Jowett's public-domain translation.
